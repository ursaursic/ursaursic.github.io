A mechanical ratchet drives unilateral cytokinesis

10.1038/s41586-023-06494-6

This is a cool Nature paper together with the lead author Alison Kickuth. It describes the mechanical mechanisms underlying unilateral cell division. My part of the project was to measure and analyze the material properties of the cytoplasm. We have shown that the changes in material properties of the cytoplasm allow the actin band to form properly during interphase and is able to ingress in M-phase.

Role: Lead researcher

From coffee stains to uniform deposits: Significance of the contact-line mobility

10.1038/s41586-023-06495-5

This project explores the role of contact-line mobility in the formation of uniform deposits. We investigate the physical principles governing the spreading and drying of liquid droplets on solid surfaces.
